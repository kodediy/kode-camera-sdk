camera_drv
==========

The drivers, one folder each: the fingerprint, the OV5640 over SCCB, the DVP and its buffers, the JPEG encoder, the PPA, the 5 MP still, the pixels, the registers, the white balance, the autofocus, EXIF and the movie writer.

Depends on: camera_bsp, espressif/esp_cam_sensor ==2.6.0, espressif/esp_sccb_intf ==0.0.9; kode-sdk, from the app's own pin

Docs: https://docs.kode.diy/modules/camera
